## Context

An organization needed one place for its training: a programme catalogue,
sessions, requests, approvals, attendance and certificates, replacing an older
system whose data was migrated in. A product owner shaped the requirements and
the screen mockups. Every engineering role was mine.

## The system

- **Application:** .NET 10 Clean Architecture with CQRS on PostgreSQL, a
  separate worker for scheduled jobs, and a Next.js front end behind a BFF, so
  tokens never reach the browser.
- **Integration:** single sign-on with Entra ID, user sync from the HR system,
  an email queue with retries.
- **Delivery:** a self-hosted GitLab with its own runner, CI/CD to separate
  development, test and production environments, tagged releases with
  generated notes.
- **Operations:** structured logs kept in the database and archived on a
  schedule; tiered backups with a stated recovery point and restores that are
  rehearsed; a daily read-only health check of production.
- **Rules:** every domain rule written as a numbered invariant, each tied to a
  test, with the reasoning recorded as a decision record.

## How a task moves

Speed came from the cycle, not from skipping steps. Every change passes the
same gates, with a review between each.

## Proof before go-live

Load- and stress-tested up to 300 concurrent users with no server errors and a
95th percentile under 400 ms. Scanned with SonarQube, OWASP ZAP and a
dependency audit.

## Outcome

In production since the third month and used by around 400 people, with
nothing since go-live beyond bug fixes and cosmetic changes. More than 2,000
automated tests guard it, and when something does go wrong it ends as a rule, a
test and a decision record.
