## Context

A large delivery team, a UI that changed weekly, and a test suite nobody had
time to extend. Every regression was found by a person.

## Decision

Put the repetition on tools and keep the judgement with people: skills that
turn a recorded session into a stable Playwright test, digest a day of logs
into the three defects that matter, and diff two environments' databases
into one report.
